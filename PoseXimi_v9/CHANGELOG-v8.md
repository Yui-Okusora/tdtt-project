# PoseXimi v8 — UX Improvements

Based on PoseXimi v7. Prior layouts and palette/theme system preserved with UX-focused refinements.

- New `css/ux-v8.css` loaded last and cached for PWA.
- Clear upload progress status and one contextual CTA on Home.
- Always-available pose illustration preview, separated from optional AI photo composition.
- 3-second hands-free camera countdown.
- Result side-by-side comparison on desktop, tab switcher on mobile.
- Search/filter/sort in local gallery.
- Mobile visual hierarchy, touch sizing, keyboard focus and reduced-motion refinements.
- Full research/audit/QA plan in `UX_RESEARCH_AND_PLAN.md`.

Known limits: production AI backend, real multi-user cloud auth, IndexedDB account isolation, camera device testing, cross-palette contrast and full browser E2E testing are separate work items, not implied complete by this release.
